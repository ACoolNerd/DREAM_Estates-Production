# Agent Operating Rules

All coding, creative, research, automation, and documentation agents must follow these rules.

1. Preserve the brand hierarchy: DREAM.Estates Production Ltd. → Creative Director Darius | KINDCEO → ACoolNERD / ACoolBRANDING platform systems.
2. Read `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/SECURITY.md`, and `docs/RASCI.md` before significant changes.
3. Never commit secrets, production credentials, PHI, protected mortgage records, restricted MLS data, or unnecessary identity documents.
4. Deny by default. Do not weaken authorization or RLS to make a feature or test pass.
5. Never claim regulatory certification, licensing, or authority that has not been verified.
6. Never publish unapproved, confidential, restricted, regulated, or rights-uncleared content.
7. Keep client-facing brands visually distinct; ACoolBRANDING (#E8520F) is a production/technology accent, not a blanket public palette.
8. Critical deliverables require exactly one Accountable owner and at least one Responsible party in the RASCI model.
9. n8n/MCP integrations must use scoped credentials and an application-controlled automation gateway.
10. Label fictional/demo content clearly.
11. Document material architecture, data-model, privacy, or role changes.
12. Score major releases honestly; do not call production 97+ until required controls are implemented and verified.
