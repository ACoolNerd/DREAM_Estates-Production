# RASCI Governance

R = Responsible · A = Accountable · S = Supporting · C = Consulted · I = Informed.

## Rules
- Every critical deliverable has exactly one **A**.
- Every critical deliverable has at least one **R**.
- Authority must reflect actual contracts, licenses, organizational roles, and approvals.
- Creative approval, legal approval, privacy approval, and regulated authority are distinct concepts.

## Default leadership
- **DREAM.Estates Executive Sponsor / Brand Owner** — business accountability where assigned.
- **Darius | KINDCEO** — Creative Director; accountable or consulted for creative direction based on project authority.
- **ACoolNERD / ACoolBRANDING** — platform architecture, production systems, automation, and technical delivery.
- **Producer / Project Lead** — day-to-day orchestration and delivery.
- **Rights / Privacy Reviewer** — release/privacy decisions when formally assigned.
- **Legal / Compliance Reviewer** — legal/regulatory decisions only when qualified and authorized.
