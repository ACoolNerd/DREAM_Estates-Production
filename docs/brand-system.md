## TYPOGRAPHY

### Typeface Roles

| Role | Style |
|---|---|
| Display / Headline | Elegant editorial serif |
| Subheading / Label | Widely tracked uppercase sans-serif |
| Body | Restrained, legible body typography |

### Typography Principles

- Generous white space
- Intentional hierarchy
- Restraint over decoration
- Letterspacing used purposefully in all-caps settings

## DESIGN GUARDRAILS

### Avoid

- Generic startup design aesthetics
- Excessive gradients
- Excessive orange on customer-facing Gia Michèle materials
- Dense copy without breathing room
- Low-contrast typography

### Embrace

- Quiet confidence over loudness
- Material photography over pure illustration
- White space as a luxury signal
- Considered editorial composition
