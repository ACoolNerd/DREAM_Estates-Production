## TRADE PROGRAM POSITIONING

The designer is not merely a discount customer.

The designer is:

- **Specifier** — selects and recommends products for projects
- **Creative Partner** — collaborates on the design narrative
- **Distribution Partner** — extends Gia Michèle's reach through projects
- **Relationship Owner** — controls the client relationship
- **Repeat Purchaser** — returns across multiple projects
- **Brand Advocate** — represents the brand within their network

> **The platform should make designers more capable.**

## TRADE MESSAGING PRINCIPLES

### What to Communicate

- We make you more capable, not just more profitable
- Your reputation is protected at every stage
- We handle the complexity so you can focus on the relationship
- Our platform is built around how designers actually work

### What to Avoid

- Positioning furniture primarily on price
- Transactional-only messaging
- Language that reduces the designer to a coupon holder
- Complicated processes that add friction
